A Kannada Learning Collective for Global Kannadigas. We believe language grows best through real conversation, shared activities, classroom participation, and cultural connection.

Now welcoming student enrollment and teacher interest for our in-person Kannada program in Frisco.

For Students

Children learn Kannada through conversation, stories, songs, games, reading, writing, and cultural connection in a warm community setting.

  • Four skill-based levels
  • In-person classes
  • Confidence-building through participation
  • Level 4 may help students explore foreign language credit through local ISDs

For Teachers

If you enjoy working with children and want to help keep Kannada alive through stories, songs, conversation, and community, we would love to hear from you.

  • Warm, collaborative teaching environment
  • In-person classroom community
  • Opportunity to shape heritage language learning
