What We Do

Kannada that children can use with family

Speak at home

Family-first Kannada

ಅಜ್ಜಿ, ನೀವು ಹೇಗಿದ್ದೀರಿ?

Grandma, how are you?

Students learn words, phrases, and sentence patterns they can use with parents, grandparents, and relatives.

Build confidence

Useful before perfect

We value participation, listening, and spoken confidence before expecting polished reading or writing.

Stay connected

Language with roots

Stories, songs, festivals, food, and family memories help Kannada feel personal instead of academic.

How It Works

Speaking first. Writing next.

01

Listen

Children hear Kannada through songs, stories, classroom routines, and repeated family phrases.

02

Speak

They practise short responses, everyday sentences, and conversations they can try at home.

03

Read

Letters and words are introduced when students are ready to connect sounds to script.

04

Write

Writing grows from familiar words into sentences, paragraphs, reflections, and presentations.
