Who We Are

A community built around language, identity, and belonging

We are a community of parents and educators committed to helping children learn Kannada and stay connected to the language, culture, and traditions of Karnataka. Our goal is to create a warm and welcoming space where children can build confidence in speaking, understanding, reading, and enjoying Kannada.

Why Kannada Shaale

Keeping Kannada alive in everyday family life

For many families living outside Karnataka, children may hear Kannada at home but have fewer chances to use it regularly. Kannada Shaale helps bridge that gap through structured learning, conversation practice, stories, songs, and cultural activities that make the language meaningful and enjoyable.

How Learning Happens

Practical, joyful, and level-appropriate

Students are grouped by level so instruction can match their current skills and confidence. Lessons may include conversation, reading, writing, stories, songs, games, and cultural learning. We aim to make Kannada feel approachable, useful, and joyful.

  • Skill-based placement, not age alone
  • Interactive lessons and repetition-based learning
  • Language and culture taught together
  • Support for confidence-building at every stage
