Level 1 — ಮಾತು ಕಲಿ

No prior Kannada needed. Listening and speaking readiness through songs, games, and routines.

Learner Profile

This level is for students who are new to Kannada or not yet comfortable responding to spoken Kannada. Students may understand a few words at home, but they do not need prior speaking, reading, or writing skills.

Prerequisites

  • No prior Kannada required
  • Comfortable joining songs, actions, games, or story time with teacher support
  • Able to participate in short group routines

Goal

Introduce Kannada in a fun and engaging way while building listening and speaking confidence. No reading or writing at this level.

Speaking

Simple self-referencing sentences using ನಾನು, ನನ್ನ, ನನಗೆ

Basic action words: ಬಾ, ಹೋಗು, ಓಡು, ಕುಡಿ, ತಿನ್ನು

Listening

  • Simple rhymes and songs
  • Short stories of 3–6 lines

Vocabulary Themes

  • Days of the week
  • Fruits and vegetables
  • Body parts
  • Animals and birds
  • Colors

Class Format — 45 Minutes

BlockDurationActivity
115 minRhymes and songs
215 minVocabulary building
315 minStory time

Ready for Level 2 When

  • Recognizes familiar classroom words and routines
  • Can repeat common words and short phrases
  • Can answer simple questions with gestures, single words, or rehearsed phrases
  • Shows readiness to begin Kannada letters and tracing
Class routines remain consistent each week to build familiarity. New learners thrive on repetition.
