Curriculum

Level 5 — ಕನ್ನಡ ಮಾತುಗಾರರು

Paragraph-ready · Presentations, essays, and free expression · 60–75 minute sessions

Learner Profile

This level is for students who can read short passages, write connected sentences, and express ideas in Kannada with growing independence.

Prerequisites

  • Reads short paragraphs with familiar vocabulary
  • Writes connected sentences with a clear idea
  • Can discuss familiar topics in Kannada
  • Can retell stories, describe experiences, or prepare short spoken pieces

Goal

Communicate ideas fluently in both spoken and written Kannada.

Reading

  • Short stories
  • Poems
  • Simple articles

Writing

  • Paragraph writing
  • Short essays
  • Story writing
  • Personal reflections

Speaking

  • Presentations
  • Storytelling
  • Conversations and debates

Class Format — 60–75 Minutes

Block Duration Activity
1 20 min Reading and discussion
2 20 min Writing activity
3 20–30 min Speaking and presentations

Growth Targets

  • Speak with confidence in presentations, storytelling, and discussions
  • Write paragraphs, short essays, and personal reflections
  • Read stories, poems, and simple articles with increasing independence
  • Use Kannada as a living language for identity, family connection, and community participation
