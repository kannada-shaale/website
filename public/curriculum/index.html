Curriculum Overview

Curriculum

Five skill-based levels — from first sounds to fluent expression.

Our curriculum is built around the reality that Kannada kids abroad learn differently from children growing up in Karnataka. Placement is based on skills, readiness, and confidence rather than age alone.

Each level has a clear learner profile: what the student should already be comfortable doing, what they will practise next, and what they should be ready for before moving forward.

View the full curriculum presentation for a visual overview of all five levels.
