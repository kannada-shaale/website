Curriculum

Level 2 — ಅಕ್ಷರ ಕಲಿ

Ready for letters · Swaras and vyanjanas · 45-minute sessions

Learner Profile

This level is for students who can participate in simple spoken Kannada routines and are ready to connect Kannada sounds to written letters.

Prerequisites

  • Can follow basic classroom instructions with support
  • Can repeat familiar Kannada words and short phrases
  • Shows readiness for pencil work, tracing, and letter recognition

Goal

Introduce Kannada alphabets and build foundational vocabulary.

Reading and Writing

Learn Kannada letters:

  • Swaras: ಅ to ಅಃ
  • Vyanjanas: ಕ to ಳ

Practice 10 simple words for each letter. No kagunita or ottakshara at this level.

Speaking and Listening

Pronouns: ನೀನು, ನಿನ್ನ, ನೀವು, ನಿಮ್ಮ

Action words: ಓದು (read), ಬರಿ (write), ನೋಡು (see), ಕೇಳು (listen), ಮಲಗು (sleep), ಮಾತಾಡು (talk)

Skills

  • Understand simple stories
  • Narrate 1–2 sentence responses

Vocabulary Topics

  • Numbers (1–20)
  • Time
  • Colors
  • Tastes
  • Family members

Class Format — 45 Minutes

Block Duration Activity
1 20 min Speaking and listening
2 20 min Reading and writing
3 5 min Games and activities

Ready for Level 3 When

  • Recognizes most swaras and vyanjanas
  • Can read and write simple letters and familiar words with support
  • Can use basic pronouns and action words in short phrases
  • Can respond to simple story or picture prompts in 1–2 sentences
