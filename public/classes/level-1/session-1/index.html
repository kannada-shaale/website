Session 1 — Hello Kannada!

Our very first session — songs, greetings and colours

Session 1 11 January 2026 Level 1

What We Covered

This week was all about making Kannada feel familiar and fun. We started with our welcome song which we’ll sing every week — it is already becoming a favourite!

Songs and Rhymes

  • Welcome song — we will sing this every week to open class
  • ಮಳೆ ಮಳೆ ಬಾ ಬಾ (Rain song) — great for actions and movement

New Vocabulary — Colors

Kannada Pronunciation English
ಕೆಂಪು kempu Red
ನೀಲಿ neeli Blue
ಹಳದಿ haladi Yellow
ಹಸಿರು hasiru Green
ಬಿಳಿ bili White

Story Time

We listened to a short story about a little elephant learning the colours of the jungle. Ask your child to tell you what colours the elephant saw!

Homework

This week’s practice (10 minutes is enough):

  1. Point to things around the house and ask your child the colour in Kannada
  2. Sing the rain song together — the actions make it easy to remember
  3. Try the sentence: ಇದು ಕೆಂಪು (ida kempu) — “This is red”

Take-Home Card

Download this week’s vocabulary card and stick it on the fridge.

For parents: Even 5–10 minutes of Kannada at home each week makes a real difference to your child’s confidence on Saturdays. You don’t need to know Kannada yourself — just ask your child to teach you!
