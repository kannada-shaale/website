Session 1 — New Structure, New Term

Introducing the co-teaching model and our year-end showcase

Session 1 11 January 2026 Level 3

What We Covered

This was a transition session introducing a new structure for the term. We also announced the year-end showcase and voted on our topic.

Showcase Vote Result

The class voted and chose Option 2 — A Day in a Kannadiga Family for our year-end performance. Congratulations to everyone for a great discussion about why this topic matters to them.

Vocabulary — Home and Family Life (Theme 1)

This month’s word bank. These 10 words will appear in every session this month:

Kannada Pronunciation English
ಮನೆ mane home
ಅಡುಗೆ aduge cooking
ಹಬ್ಬ habba festival
ಊಟ oota meal
ಅಜ್ಜಿ ajji grandmother
ತಾತ tata grandfather
ಸಂತೋಷ santosha happiness
ಒಟ್ಟಿಗೆ ottige together
ಸ್ವಾದಿಷ್ಟ svaadishta delicious
ನೆನಪು nenapu memory

Pair Work

Students practised using the new words in sentences. Try these sentence starters at home:

  • ನಮ್ಮ ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ… (In our home…)
  • ನನ್ನ ಅಜ್ಜಿ… (My grandmother…)

Homework

  1. Practise all 10 vocabulary words — the take-home card has them with pictures
  2. Write 3 sentences using this week’s words
  3. Ask a family member: ನಿಮ್ಮ ಮೆಚ್ಚಿನ ಊಟ ಯಾವುದು? (What is your favourite meal?)

Notes for Parents

This term we are working toward a showcase performance at the community year-end event. Your child has helped choose the topic — a Kannadiga family scene. Practising the vocabulary at home each week will make a real difference to their confidence on stage.

The whole-month vocabulary list is on the take-home card. Even one conversation in Kannada at dinner using these words helps enormously.
