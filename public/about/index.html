About

Why we started, what we believe, and who we are.

Our Mission

ನಮ್ಮ ಕನ್ನಡ ಶಾಲೆ gives Kannada kids abroad a living connection to Karnataka — not as a museum piece, but as something that belongs to them.

We are not trying to produce native speakers. We are trying to produce children who feel proud of where they come from, who can speak a few words with their grandparents, who know the stories and festivals of Karnataka, and who feel curious rather than embarrassed about their heritage.

That is a modest and achievable goal. It is also, we think, a deeply important one.

Our Approach

Children who grow up in the West have no survival reason to learn Kannada. Any teaching approach that ignores this will fail. So we build sessions around enjoyment, cultural pride, and real purpose — not grammar drills and textbook exercises.

We teach through stories, songs, conversation, and community. We build toward real performances, real conversations, real moments of connection with family members.

We also teach teachers. Running a heritage language school is genuinely hard, and most of the people doing it are volunteers without formal training. We invest in giving our teachers simple, effective tools — structured session plans, a co-teaching model, clear learner profiles — so they can focus on the relationship with their students rather than reinventing the wheel each week.
