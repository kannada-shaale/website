Who This Is For

In-person curriculum for children growing into Kannada

ಹೊಸ ಚಿಗುರು is designed for children who are learning Kannada in a community setting, with lessons built around participation, conversation, stories, songs, routines, reading, and writing. Students move through the curriculum by skill and readiness, not age alone.

  • Steady confidence-building over time
