Why Nudi

Language grows best through connection, participation, and community

Nudi exists to help children build a lasting connection to Kannada language and the culture of Karnataka. We want children to feel comfortable hearing Kannada, speaking it with confidence, and experiencing it as a living part of family and community life.

Our model is intentionally in person. Language learning at this stage depends on shared attention, group routines, songs, stories, games, classroom energy, and real conversation. Those experiences help children participate naturally, build confidence, and form positive associations with the language in ways that are difficult to recreate in a virtual-only setting.

Level 4 Outcome

Students who complete Level 4 may be ready to pursue foreign language credit through local ISDs

Our four-level pathway is designed to build toward real language confidence. By the end of Level 4, students may be prepared to explore foreign language credit options offered through their local school districts, depending on district policies and evaluation requirements.

How Learning Happens

Structured, joyful, and designed for real participation

Students are grouped by level so instruction can match their current skills and confidence. Lessons include conversation, stories, songs, games, cultural learning, reading, and writing based on the learner’s stage. The goal is to make Kannada feel approachable, useful, and enjoyable while steadily building skill over time.

  • Skill-based placement, not age alone
  • Interactive, in-person lessons with repetition and routine
  • Language and culture taught together
  • Support for confidence-building at every stage
Who We Are

A parent and educator community committed to heritage learning

We are a community of parents and educators working together to help children stay connected to Kannada with warmth, consistency, and purpose. Our focus is not only on language skills, but also on identity, belonging, and a sense of pride in cultural heritage.
