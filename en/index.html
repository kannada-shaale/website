Why ಹೊಸ ಚಿಗುರು

Curriculum built for expat Kannadiga children

ಹೊಸ ಚಿಗುರು designs practical, joyful Kannada curriculum for children growing up outside Karnataka. We want communities to have a clear path for helping children hear Kannada, speak it with confidence, and experience it as a living part of family and cultural life.

Our model is intentionally built for in-person learning environments. At this stage, language grows through shared attention, stories, songs, routines, classroom energy, and real conversation. Those experiences are difficult to recreate well in virtual-only settings, which is why ಹೊಸ ಚಿಗುರು is designed for community-based, in-person adoption.

First Adoption Site

Frisco Hanuman Temple is the first community to adopt the ಹೊಸ ಚಿಗುರು curriculum

ಹೊಸ ಚಿಗುರು is being put into practice first through the Bala Datta program at Karya Siddhi Hanuman Temple in Frisco. We hope this becomes a model for other communities that want a thoughtful, in-person Kannada curriculum for expat children.

How It Works

Structured curriculum communities can adopt locally

ಹೊಸ ಚಿಗುರು is designed as a complete in-person curriculum pathway that communities can adopt and teach locally. Lessons include conversation, stories, songs, games, cultural learning, reading, and writing based on the learner’s stage. The goal is to make Kannada feel approachable, useful, and enjoyable while steadily building skill over time.

  • Skill-based progression, not age alone
  • Designed for interactive, in-person instruction
  • Language and culture taught together
  • A reusable structure for community adoption
Who We Are

An organization focused on heritage-language curriculum

ಹೊಸ ಚಿಗುರು brings together parents, educators, and community builders who care about heritage-language learning.
