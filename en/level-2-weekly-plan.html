Level 2 Weekly Plan

First 8 weeks for ಅಕ್ಷರ ಕಲಿ, combining spoken Kannada with swaras, vyanjanas, simple words, and beginning kagunita awareness.

Class Structure

  • 10 min: spoken warm-up and review
  • 15 min: letter focus
  • 10 min: word practice
  • 10 min: game, reading practice, or mini activity
  • 5 min: wrap-up and home task

Weeks 1-8

Week 1

Welcome Back to Kannada + First Letters

Goal: settle into routines and learn ಅ, ಆ.

Vocabulary: ಅಮ್ಮ, ಆನೆ

Home practice: find ಅ and ಆ on the alphabet page.

Week 2

More Swarās

Goal: recognize and say ಇ, ಈ, ಉ, ಊ.

Vocabulary: ಇಲಿ, ಈಚೆ, ಉಪ್ಪು, ಊರು

Home practice: review and say these letters aloud.

Week 3

E and O Family Swarās

Goal: recognize and say ಎ, ಏ, ಐ, ಒ, ಓ, ಔ.

Vocabulary: ಎಲೆ, ಏಣಿ, ಐದು, ಒಂಟೆ, ಓಟ, ಔಷಧ

Home practice: review all swaras learned so far.

Week 4

First Consonants

Goal: identify the first consonants ಕ and ಗ.

Vocabulary: ಕಮಲ, ಕಾಯಿ, ಗಿಡ, ಗಜ

Home practice: find words beginning with ಕ and ಗ.

Week 5

More Consonants

Goal: learn ಚ, ಜ, ಟ, ಡ.

Vocabulary: ಚಪ್ಪಲಿ, ಜಮಖಾನ, ಟಮೋಟ, ಡಬ್ಬಿ

Home practice: bring one word for one letter next class.

Week 6

Everyday Consonants

Goal: identify and say ತ, ದ, ನ, ಪ, ಬ, ಮ.

Vocabulary: ತಾತ, ದೋಸೆ, ನಾಯಿ, ಪುಸ್ತಕ, ಬಾಳೆಹಣ್ಣು, ಮಣ್ಣು

Home practice: say 3 words and identify their first letter.

Week 7

Bringing Letters Together

Goal: read letters more quickly and build confidence with simple words.

Practice words: ಅಮ್ಮ, ಆನೆ, ನಾಯಿ, ಗಿಡ

Home practice: read 5 letter cards and 3 words.

Week 8

Beginning Kagunita Awareness

Goal: notice the pattern in the ಕ row: ಕ ಕಾ ಕಿ ಕೀ ಕು ಕೂ ಕೆ ಕೇ.

Home practice: review the ಕ row with a parent using the kagunita page.
