Level 2 Weekly Plan

A teacher-ready 8-week plan for ಅಕ್ಷರ ಕಲಿ, combining spoken Kannada with swaras, vyanjanas, simple words, and beginning kagunita awareness.

Teacher Snapshot

Who This Is For

  • Children who can follow simple Kannada routines
  • Children ready to connect sounds to letters

Teaching Focus

  • Keep speaking confidence active
  • Connect sound, symbol, and meaning
  • Prioritize patterns over memorizing shapes

Suggested Class Flow

  • 8 min spoken warm-up
  • 12 min letter introduction
  • 10 min tracing or matching
  • 10 min simple reading game
  • 5-10 min recap and home task

Keep Ready

  • Alphabet cards
  • Picture cards
  • Tracing tray or sheet
  • Pocket chart or floor cards

Weeks 1-8

Week 1

Welcome Back to Kannada + First Letters

Theme: classroom routines and first swaras

Objectives

  • Recognize ಅ and ಆ
  • Say the sounds clearly
  • Connect each letter to one anchor word

Letters and Words

ಅ, ಆ
ಅಮ್ಮ, ಆನೆ

Class Flow

  1. Begin with greetings and spoken review.
  2. Introduce ಅ and ಆ.
  3. Connect each one to a picture word.
  4. Air-write or finger-trace both letters.
  5. Match letter cards to pictures.

Teacher Prompts

ಇದು ಯಾವ ಅಕ್ಷರ?
ಅ ಅಮ್ಮ
ಆ ಆನೆ

Quick check: child distinguishes ಅ and ಆ.

Home practice: find both letters on the alphabet page.

Week 2

More Swarās

Theme: short and long vowel pairs

Objectives

  • Recognize ಇ, ಈ, ಉ, ಊ
  • Hear short and long sounds
  • Match letters to familiar words

Letters and Words

ಇ, ಈ, ಉ, ಊ
ಇಲಿ, ಈಚೆ, ಉಪ್ಪು, ಊರು

Class Flow

  1. Review ಅ and ಆ.
  2. Teach ಇ/ಈ and ಉ/ಊ as pairs.
  3. Model short and long sounds clearly.
  4. Sort pictures under the correct letter.
  5. Echo-read all four letters in rhythm.

Teacher Prompts

ಚಿಕ್ಕ ಧ್ವನಿ
ಉದ್ದ ಧ್ವನಿ

Quick check: child repeats all four sounds and matches two pictures.

Home practice: say one word for each letter.

Week 3

E and O Family Swarās

Theme: expanding the swara family

Objectives

  • Recognize ಎ, ಏ, ಐ, ಒ, ಓ, ಔ
  • Listen carefully to related sound groups
  • Review older swaras

Letters and Words

ಎಲೆ, ಏಣಿ, ಐದು, ಒಂಟೆ, ಓಟ, ಔಷಧ

Class Flow

  1. Review all earlier swaras.
  2. Teach new swaras in small related groups.
  3. Do listen-and-point activities.
  4. Match pictures to first sounds.
  5. Read the swara strip together.

Teacher Prompts

ಕೇಳಿ ಮತ್ತು ತೋರಿಸು
ಯಾವ ಧ್ವನಿ ಕೇಳಿದೆ?

Quick check: child points to the right swara when hearing the sound.

Home practice: review all swaras for 3-5 minutes.

Week 4

First Consonants

Theme: beginning vyanjanas with familiar words

Objectives

  • Identify ಕ and ಗ
  • Connect consonants to known words
  • Notice beginning sounds

Letters and Words

ಕ, ಗ
ಕಮಲ, ಕಾಯಿ, ಗಿಡ, ಗಜ

Class Flow

  1. Review swaras briefly.
  2. Introduce ಕ and ಗ.
  3. Repeat sound, then word.
  4. Match pictures by first sound.
  5. Trace both letters.

Teacher Prompts

ಇದು ಕ
ಇದು ಗ
ಗ ಎಲ್ಲಿ ಇದೆ?

Quick check: child identifies both letters side by side.

Home practice: find words beginning with ಕ and ಗ.

Week 5

More Consonants

Theme: adding ಚ, ಜ, ಟ, ಡ

Objectives

  • Identify four new consonants
  • Hear the first sound in familiar words
  • Keep earlier consonants active

Letters and Words

ಚ, ಜ, ಟ, ಡ
ಚಪ್ಪಲಿ, ಜಮಖಾನ, ಟಮೋಟ, ಡಬ್ಬಿ

Class Flow

  1. Review ಕ and ಗ.
  2. Teach the four new letters with picture anchors.
  3. Repeat sound and word together.
  4. Play a first-sound matching game.
  5. Mix old and new letters for quick review.

Teacher Prompts

ಮೊದಲ ಅಕ್ಷರ ಯಾವುದು?
ಕೇಳಿ ಮತ್ತು ಹೊಂದಿಸು

Quick check: child identifies at least 2 of the 4 new letters.

Home practice: bring one matching word next class.

Week 6

Everyday Consonants

Theme: high-use consonants in common words

Objectives

  • Identify ತ, ದ, ನ, ಪ, ಬ, ಮ
  • Say the first sound before the full word
  • Build confidence with common beginnings

Letters and Words

ತಾತ, ದೋಸೆ, ನಾಯಿ, ಪುಸ್ತಕ, ಬಾಳೆಹಣ್ಣು, ಮಣ್ಣು

Class Flow

  1. Review earlier consonants.
  2. Teach new letters in small groups.
  3. Say the sound, then the word.
  4. Match pictures to first letters.
  5. Run a rapid review at the end.

Teacher Prompts

ಮೊದಲು ಅಕ್ಷರ ಹೇಳು
ಈಗ ಸಂಪೂರ್ಣ ಪದ ಹೇಳು

Quick check: child identifies at least 3 of the 6 letters.

Home practice: say 3 words and identify the first letter.

Week 7

Bringing Letters Together

Theme: reading simple familiar words

Objectives

  • Review all taught letters
  • Build faster recognition
  • Experience success reading simple words

Practice Words

ಅಮ್ಮ, ಆನೆ, ನಾಯಿ, ಗಿಡ

Class Flow

  1. Review letters with a flash round.
  2. Show how letters combine into a word.
  3. Read simple words slowly as a group.
  4. Rebuild one word with cards.
  5. Support each child through one word attempt.

Teacher Prompts

ಅಕ್ಷರಗಳನ್ನು ಸೇರಿಸೋಣ
ನಿಧಾನವಾಗಿ ಓದು

Quick check: child reads or repeats one simple word.

Home practice: read 5 letter cards and 3 simple words.

Week 8

Beginning Kagunita Awareness

Theme: noticing the ಕ row pattern

Objectives

  • Understand that one consonant changes with vowel sounds
  • Say the ಕ row with support
  • Notice the visual pattern without pressure

Focus Row

ಕ ಕಾ ಕಿ ಕೀ ಕು ಕೂ ಕೆ ಕೇ

Class Flow

  1. Review the base letter ಕ.
  2. Show the row one form at a time.
  3. Say the row together in rhythm.
  4. Ask what stays the same and what changes.
  5. Match spoken forms to written cards.

Teacher Prompts

ಬದಲಾದ ಆಕಾರ ನೋಡಿ
ಒಂದೇ ಅಕ್ಷರ, ಬೇರೆ ಧ್ವನಿ

Quick check: child understands that the base letter stays the same.

Home practice: review the ಕ row with a parent.
