Level 1 Weekly Plan

A teacher-ready 8-week plan for ಮಾತು ಕಲಿ, designed for children who are building listening comfort and early spoken Kannada.

This Week

Combined Level 1 + Level 2 Class — 20 / 20 / 20

Keep both levels together for the first 40 minutes, then split for focused practice. The goal is for every child to willingly say at least one Kannada phrase.

0–20 minutes · Together

Fast Introduction Game

Game: Kannada Four Corners

  1. Post four picture signs: name, town, parents, and grade.
  2. Children move while music plays. When it stops, they go to the called corner and find a partner.
  3. Partners ask and answer that corner's question, switch roles, and move again.
  4. Level 2 uses full sentences; Level 1 may begin with one-word answers.

ನಿನ್ನ ಹೆಸರೇನು? — ನನ್ನ ಹೆಸರು ___

ನಿನ್ನ ಊರು ಯಾವುದು? — ನನ್ನ ಊರು ___

ನಿನ್ನ ಅಮ್ಮ/ಅಪ್ಪನ ಹೆಸರೇನು? — ನನ್ನ ಅಮ್ಮ/ಅಪ್ಪನ ಹೆಸರು ___

ನೀನು ಯಾವ ತರಗತಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಓದುತ್ತೀಯ? — ನಾನು ___ನೇ ತರಗತಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ಓದುತ್ತೇನೆ

Let children pass or use a pretend character if they do not want to share personal information.

20–40 minutes · Together

Echo Songs and Actions

Teacher sings one line, children echo it, then everyone adds actions. Repeat once softly, once loudly, and once quickly.

Introduction song
ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ, ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ
ನನ್ನ ಹೆಸರು ______
ನಿನ್ನ ಹೆಸರು ಏನು?
ನಾವು ಎಲ್ಲರೂ ಸ್ನೇಹಿತರು!

Action rhyme
ಕೈ ಮೇಲಕ್ಕೆ, ಕೈ ಕೆಳಕ್ಕೆ
ಒಂದು ಚಪ್ಪಾಳೆ ತಟ್ಟು
ಬಲಕ್ಕೆ ತಿರುಗು, ಎಡಕ್ಕೆ ತಿರುಗು
ನಗುತ್ತಾ ಕುಳಿತುಕೋ!

40–60 minutes · Split

Level-Specific Practice

Level 1 — speaking:

  • Play “Teacher Says” with ಬಾ, ಕುಳಿತುಕೋ, ಎದ್ದು ನಿಲ್ಲು, and ಕೈ ತಟ್ಟು.
  • Use a puppet to ask each child ನಿನ್ನ ಹೆಸರೇನು?
  • Exit phrase: ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ or ನನ್ನ ಹೆಸರು ___.

Level 2 — write ಅ and ಆ:

  • 2 min: review ಅಮ್ಮ and ಆನೆ.
  • 4 min: teacher models both letters.
  • 4 min: air-write and finger-trace.
  • 7 min: trace three and independently write three of each.
  • 3 min: hear ಅ or ಆ, write it, and hold it up.

Teacher Snapshot

Who This Is For

  • Children new to Kannada
  • Children who understand a little but rarely speak

Teaching Focus

  • Listening first
  • Speaking next
  • Joyful repetition over perfect recall

Suggested Class Flow

  • 5 min arrival routine
  • 8 min song and spoken warm-up
  • 10 min new words
  • 10 min speaking game
  • 7 min story or rhyme
  • 5 min wrap-up and parent handoff

Keep Ready

  • Name tags
  • Picture cards
  • Soft ball or bean bag
  • Crayons and paper

Weeks 1-8

Week 1

Hello Kannada

Theme: greetings and classroom comfort

Objectives

  • Children feel safe entering class
  • Children respond to their own name
  • Children repeat basic greetings

Vocabulary

ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ, ಹಾಯ್, ಬಾ, ಕುಳಿತುಕೋ, ಎದ್ದು ನಿಲ್ಲು, ಹೆಸರು

Class Flow

  1. Welcome each child with ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ.
  2. Sing a hello song and wave to each child.
  3. Model ನನ್ನ ಹೆಸರು ... and repeat.
  4. Play a ball toss name game.
  5. Give simple action commands.

Teacher Prompts

ನಿನ್ನ ಹೆಸರು ಏನು?
ನನ್ನ ಹೆಸರು ...

Quick check: child responds to name and repeats one greeting.

Home practice: greet family with ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ.

Week 2

Colors Around Us

Theme: colors in familiar objects

Objectives

  • Say 4-5 colors
  • Answer ಇದು ಯಾವ ಬಣ್ಣ?
  • Connect colors to real objects

Vocabulary

ಕೆಂಪು, ನೀಲಿ, ಹಳದಿ, ಹಸಿರು, ಬಿಳಿ

Class Flow

  1. Review greetings from Week 1.
  2. Teach each color with a real object.
  3. Run a classroom color hunt.
  4. Sort cards or objects by color.
  5. End with each child naming one object color.

Teacher Prompts

ಇದು ಯಾವ ಬಣ್ಣ?
ಇದು ಕೆಂಪು

Quick check: child points correctly and says 2 color words.

Home practice: name colors of toys or clothes at home.

Week 3

Family Words

Theme: people children speak to most

Objectives

  • Identify close family members in Kannada
  • Answer simple family questions
  • Use ನನ್ನ ... with support

Vocabulary

ಅಮ್ಮ, ಅಪ್ಪ, ಅಣ್ಣ, ಅಕ್ಕ, ಅಜ್ಜಿ, ತಾತ

Class Flow

  1. Warm up with greetings and color review.
  2. Show family picture cards and name each one.
  3. Repeat and point.
  4. Match spoken words to pictures.
  5. Invite children to share one family word.

Teacher Prompts

ಇದು ಯಾರು?
ಇದು ಅಮ್ಮ

Quick check: child identifies ಅಮ್ಮ and ಅಪ್ಪ.

Home practice: name 3 family members in Kannada.

Week 4

Fruits We Know

Theme: favorite fruits and simple preferences

Objectives

  • Name 4-5 familiar fruits
  • Begin using ನನಗೆ ... ಇಷ್ಟ
  • Answer simple choice questions

Vocabulary

ಸೇಬು, ಬಾಳೆಹಣ್ಣು, ದ್ರಾಕ್ಷಿ, ಮಾವು, ಕಿತ್ತಳೆ

Class Flow

  1. Review family words quickly.
  2. Introduce fruits with pictures or toys.
  3. Let children choose a fruit and name it.
  4. Model ನನಗೆ ... ಇಷ್ಟ.
  5. Do a favorite fruit circle share.

Teacher Prompts

ನಿನಗೆ ಏನು ಇಷ್ಟ?
ನನಗೆ ಬಾಳೆಹಣ್ಣು ಇಷ್ಟ

Quick check: child names one fruit and completes the sentence with support.

Home practice: name fruits during snack time.

Week 5

Animals and Sounds

Theme: common animals and playful sound cues

Objectives

  • Identify 4-6 common animals
  • Connect animal names with movement or sounds
  • Speak more freely through play

Vocabulary

ನಾಯಿ, ಬೆಕ್ಕು, ಆನೆ, ಹಸು, ಹಕ್ಕಿ, ಮೀನು

Class Flow

  1. Review fruit words.
  2. Show and name each animal card.
  3. Repeat with sound or movement.
  4. Play "guess the animal."
  5. Close with each child naming one animal.

Teacher Prompts

ಇದು ಯಾವ ಪ್ರಾಣಿ?
ಇದು ನಾಯಿ

Quick check: child identifies at least 3 animals.

Home practice: say Kannada animal words while reading books.

Week 6

Body Parts

Theme: body words through action

Objectives

  • Say common body parts
  • Follow action commands in Kannada
  • Join physically even if speech is limited

Vocabulary

ಕಣ್ಣು, ಮೂಗು, ಬಾಯಿ, ಕಿವಿ, ಕೈ, ಕಾಲು

Class Flow

  1. Review animals with a movement warm-up.
  2. Model each body part by pointing.
  3. Children touch and repeat.
  4. Play a Kannada Simon Says game.
  5. Finish with partner pointing commands.

Teacher Prompts

ಕಣ್ಣು ತೋರಿಸು
ಕೈ ತಟ್ಟೋಣ

Quick check: child follows 2 commands and says 2 body-part words.

Home practice: point and say body parts at home.

Week 7

Food and Tastes

Theme: familiar foods and simple opinions

Objectives

  • Talk about favorite foods
  • Use simple taste or preference words
  • Answer personal questions with support

Vocabulary

ಊಟ, ಹಾಲು, ಅನ್ನ, ದೋಸೆ, ಸಿಹಿ, ಖಾರ

Class Flow

  1. Review body parts quickly.
  2. Introduce food picture cards.
  3. Model ನನಗೆ ... ಇಷ್ಟ.
  4. Sort foods into simple groups.
  5. Do a favorite food circle share.

Teacher Prompts

ನಿನಗೆ ಏನು ಇಷ್ಟ?
ಇದು ಸಿಹಿ

Quick check: child names one food and attempts a preference sentence.

Home practice: say one Kannada food word during mealtime.

Week 8

Review and Celebration

Theme: confidence, recall, and joyful speaking

Objectives

  • Review Weeks 1-7
  • Notice each child’s speaking comfort
  • Celebrate progress without pressure

Review Areas

Greetings, colors, family, fruits, animals, body parts, and food

Class Flow

  1. Begin with the hello song.
  2. Set up mixed review stations.
  3. Rotate children through naming and matching tasks.
  4. Invite a tiny share from each child.
  5. Make quick teacher notes after class.

Teacher Prompts

ನನ್ನ ಹೆಸರು ...
ನನಗೆ ... ಇಷ್ಟ

Quick check: what can the child now say independently?

Home practice: ask the child to teach one song and one sentence from class.
