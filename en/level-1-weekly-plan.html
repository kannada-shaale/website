Level 1 Weekly Plan

First 8 weeks for ಮಾತು ಕಲಿ, focused on listening, speaking, songs, movement, routines, and joyful repetition.

Class Structure

  • 10 min: welcome song, greetings, settling in
  • 10 min: theme vocabulary with actions or objects
  • 10 min: speaking practice through games
  • 10 min: story, rhyme, or song
  • 5 min: goodbye review and parent handoff

Weeks 1-8

Week 1

Hello Kannada

Goal: help children feel safe, hear greetings, and respond to their own name.

Vocabulary: ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ, ಹಾಯ್, ಬಾ, ಕುಳಿತುಕೋ, ಎದ್ದು ನಿಲ್ಲು, ಹೆಸರು

Home practice: greet family with ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ and say their name in Kannada.

Week 2

Colors Around Us

Goal: identify 4-5 colors and answer ಇದು ಯಾವ ಬಣ್ಣ?

Vocabulary: ಕೆಂಪು, ನೀಲಿ, ಹಳದಿ, ಹಸಿರು, ಬಿಳಿ

Home practice: point to objects at home and name the color in Kannada.

Week 3

Family Words

Goal: learn familiar family words and answer simple family questions.

Vocabulary: ಅಮ್ಮ, ಅಪ್ಪ, ಅಣ್ಣ, ಅಕ್ಕ, ಅಜ್ಜಿ, ತಾತ

Home practice: name 3 family members in Kannada.

Week 4

Fruits We Know

Goal: name familiar fruits and use ನನಗೆ ... ಇಷ್ಟ.

Vocabulary: ಸೇಬು, ಬಾಳೆಹಣ್ಣು, ದ್ರಾಕ್ಷಿ, ಮಾವು, ಕಿತ್ತಳೆ

Home practice: name fruits during snack time.

Week 5

Animals and Sounds

Goal: identify common animals and enjoy sound-based speaking games.

Vocabulary: ನಾಯಿ, ಬೆಕ್ಕು, ಆನೆ, ಹಸು, ಹಕ್ಕಿ, ಮೀನು

Home practice: point to animals in books and say the Kannada word.

Week 6

Body Parts

Goal: understand and say common body parts, and follow action instructions.

Vocabulary: ಕಣ್ಣು, ಮೂಗು, ಬಾಯಿ, ಕಿವಿ, ಕೈ, ಕಾಲು

Home practice: point to body parts and name them in Kannada.

Week 7

Food and Tastes

Goal: talk about favorite foods and begin expressing likes.

Vocabulary: ಊಟ, ಹಾಲು, ಅನ್ನ, ದೋಸೆ, ಸಿಹಿ, ಖಾರ

Home practice: say one Kannada food word during mealtime.

Week 8

Review and Celebration

Goal: celebrate progress and review greetings, colors, family, fruits, animals, body parts, and food.

Home practice: ask the child to teach one song, one family word, and one favorite sentence from class.
