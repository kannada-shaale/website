Teach With Us

Help make Kannada feel alive, joyful, and personal

Nudi is built by parents, volunteers, and educators who care deeply about helping children stay connected to Kannada language and culture. If you enjoy working with children, storytelling, songs, conversation, or community learning, we would love to hear from you.

You do not need to do everything alone. We value warmth, consistency, and a willingness to help children participate with confidence. If that sounds like you, please reach out.
