Kagunita Patterns

A kid-friendly guide to how Kannada vowels join with a consonant. The goal is to help children quickly spot the shape pattern, not memorize everything at once.

What is Kagunita?

Kagunita happens when a consonant joins a vowel sound. Start with a base letter like ಕ, then change the shape a little to make a new sound.

Kid trick: think of ಕ as the home letter. Each vowel adds a new “hat,” “tail,” or “side friend” to make a new sound.

Pattern Clues to Notice

Right-side helper

ಕ → ಕಾ

When you hear a long aa, look for the added tail on the right.

Below-the-letter helper

ಕ → ಕು / ಕೂ

For u and uu, look below the consonant.

Before-the-letter helper

ಕ → ಕೆ / ಕೇ / ಕೈ

For e, ee, and ai, part of the shape appears before the consonant.

Both sides together

ಕ → ಕೊ / ಕೋ / ಕೌ

For o, oo, and au, look on both sides of the letter.
