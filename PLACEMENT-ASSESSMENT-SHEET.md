Use this quick sheet to place each child in the most suitable Kannada level.

Suggested assessment time: `8-12 minutes` per child

- Does the child speak Kannada at home? `Yes / No / Sometimes`
- Has the child learned Kannada letters before? `Yes / No / Unsure`

## How To Use This Sheet

1. Start with a warm conversation so the child relaxes.
2. Check speaking/listening first.
3. Then check letter recognition, reading, and writing only if appropriate.
4. Place by demonstrated skill, not by age.

## Quick Rubric

Score each area from `0-3`.

- `0` = not yet
- `1` = emerging, needs heavy support
- `2` = mostly able with some support
- `3` = confident and independent

## Placement Guide

### Level 1: `ಮಾತು ಕಲಿ`

Place here if the child:
- is new to Kannada or only understands a few words
- responds mostly with gestures, English, or isolated Kannada words
- is not yet ready for formal reading/writing

Typical pattern:
- speaking/listening scores mostly `0-1`
- reading/writing scores mostly `0`

### Level 2: `ಅಕ್ಷರ ಕಲಿ`

Place here if the child:
- can follow simple Kannada routines
- can respond with some familiar words or short phrases
- is ready to begin or strengthen letter recognition

Typical pattern:
- speaking/listening scores around `1-2`
- letter recognition emerging
- little or no independent reading/writing

### Level 3: `ಪದ ಕಲಿ`

Place here if the child:
- recognizes many letters
- can read or sound out simple words
- can use short Kannada phrases and simple sentences
- is ready for word-building work

Typical pattern:
- letter/word scores around `2`
- sentence expression still limited

### Level 4: `ವಾಕ್ಯ ಕಲಿ`

Place here if the child:
- reads simple words and short sentences
- writes short sentences with support
- can hold a short guided conversation
- is ready for paragraph-level reading and more structured expression

Typical pattern:
- reading/writing scores around `2`
- connected expression emerging

### Level 5: `ಕನ್ನಡ ಮಾತುಗಾರರು`

Place here if the child:
- speaks comfortably in Kannada on familiar topics
- reads short passages
- writes connected sentences or short paragraphs
- can describe, explain, retell, or present ideas in Kannada

Typical pattern:
- most areas score `2-3`
- connected speaking is visible

## Quick Decision Shortcut

If you need a very fast placement:

- mostly understands but does not speak much -> `ಮಾತು ಕಲಿ`
- speaks a little, not reading yet -> `ಅಕ್ಷರ ಕಲಿ`
- knows letters, reads some words -> `ಪದ ಕಲಿ`
- reads words/sentences, writes some sentences -> `ವಾಕ್ಯ ಕಲಿ`
- speaks and explains ideas comfortably -> `ಕನ್ನಡ ಮಾತುಗಾರರು`
