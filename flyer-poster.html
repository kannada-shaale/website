Nudi Kannada Shaale

An in-person Kannada learning community for children. Language grows best through real conversation, shared activities, classroom participation, and cultural connection.

Now enrolling students and welcoming teacher interest for our in-person Kannada program.

For Students

Children learn through stories, songs, conversation, reading, writing, and cultural connection in a warm classroom setting.

  • Four skill-based levels
  • In-person classes
  • Confidence-building through participation
  • Level 4 may help students explore foreign language credit through local ISDs

For Teachers

If you enjoy working with children and want to help keep Kannada alive through stories, songs, conversation, and community, we would love to hear from you.

  • Warm and collaborative environment
  • In-person classroom community
  • A meaningful way to support heritage language learning

Why In Person Matters

Nudi is intentionally built around in-person learning. Children build confidence best when they can sing, respond, participate, and connect in a shared classroom environment.
