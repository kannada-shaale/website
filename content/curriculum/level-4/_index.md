## Learner Profile

This level is for students who can read and write simple Kannada words and sentences, and are ready for more complex reading and paragraph-level expression.

## Prerequisites

- Reads simple words and sentences using swaras, vyanjanas, and kagunitas
- Writes short sentences with familiar vocabulary
- Can participate in a short guided conversation
- Can retell a simple idea or story sequence with support

## Goal

Read and write sentences confidently and introduce ottaksharas.

## Reading and Writing

- Ottaksharas introduced
- Reading short paragraphs
- Writing 4–6 sentence paragraphs
- Sentence formation

### Reading Materials
- Simple stories
- Short cultural stories
- Folk tales

## Speaking and Listening

- Describe events and experiences
- Hold conversations (2–3 minutes)
- Retell stories

## Class Format — 60 Minutes

| Block | Duration | Activity |
|-------|----------|----------|
| 1 | 20 min | Reading practice |
| 2 | 20 min | Writing practice |
| 3 | 20 min | Speaking and discussion |

## Ready for Level 5 When

- Reads short paragraphs with familiar vocabulary
- Writes 4–6 connected sentences
- Uses ottaksharas in reading and writing with support
- Can describe events, experiences, or stories in Kannada
