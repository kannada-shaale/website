## Learner Profile

This level is for students who recognize basic Kannada letters and are ready to build words using kagunitas.

## Prerequisites

- Recognizes most swaras and vyanjanas
- Can read or sound out simple familiar words
- Can write basic letters with support
- Can understand and answer simple spoken prompts

## Goal

Introduce kagunitas and begin forming words and simple sentences.

## Reading and Writing

- Kagunitas (no ottaksharas yet)
- Word formation using kagunitas
- Reading simple words
- Writing simple sentences

### Topics
- Family relationships
- Directions
- Emotions
- Daily activities

## Speaking and Listening

- Translate simple sentences
- Hold short conversations (1–2 minutes)
- Read simple stories without ottaksharas

## Class Format — 60 Minutes

| Block | Duration | Activity |
|-------|----------|----------|
| 1 | 20 min | Review previous lessons |
| 2 | 20 min | Speaking and listening |
| 3 | 20 min | Reading and writing |

## Ready for Level 4 When

- Reads simple kagunita words with increasing confidence
- Writes simple sentences with familiar vocabulary
- Can hold a short guided conversation
- Can understand short stories without ottaksharas
