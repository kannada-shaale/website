## Books

### Script and Alphabet Practice
- **ಸ್ವರ ಬಲ್ಲ ಪುಸ್ತಕ** — excellent for learning swaras
- Kannada letter and word practice workbooks (available at Indian book stores)

### Story Books
- [Harivu Books](https://harivubooks.com/collections/kids-books-kannada) — beautifully illustrated Kannada children's books
- [Total Kannada](https://www.totalkannada.com/catalogue/6e9c8ec6-bb25-4c80-8ffb-628268d5ae9f.html?ref=menu) — curated catalogue of Kannada books for children
- [Moggu Books](https://moggubooks.com/) — Kannada books for young readers

## Apps

- **Learn Kannada App** — words, vocabulary, and common communication sentences. Good for daily practice between sessions.

## Videos

- [InfoBells Kannada Rhymes](https://www.youtube.com/@infobellskannadarhymes) — simple Kannada songs and rhymes, perfect for Level 1 and Level 2 children. Great for the car or before bed.

## Audio

- [ಕೇಳಿರೊಂದು ಕಥೆಯ](https://open.spotify.com/show/6qYK4IOdAxvshf7g8M8YAU) — Kannada audio stories for children on Spotify. Excellent for building listening comprehension without screen time.

## A Note on Home Practice

Even 10 minutes a day makes a measurable difference. The most effective things parents can do are:

- Play InfoBells songs during car journeys
- Listen to one audio story at bedtime each week
- Ask your child to teach you the words they learned on Saturday
