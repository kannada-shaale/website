# Volunteer Placement Rubric

Use this quick rubric to help place each child in the right Kannada level.

Goal: keep it simple, warm, and practical.

Suggested time per child: `5-8 minutes`

## 1. Ask These 4 Things

### A. Can the child understand simple Kannada?

Try:
- `ಬಾ`
- `ಕುಳಿತುಕೋ`
- `ಇದು ಏನು?`

Mark one:
- `Not yet`
- `A little`
- `Mostly`
- `Confident`

### B. Can the child speak in Kannada?

Try:
- `ನಿನ್ನ ಹೆಸರು ಏನು?`
- `ಹೇಗಿದ್ದೀಯ?`
- `ನಿನಗೆ ಏನು ಇಷ್ಟ?`

Mark one:
- `Single words only`
- `Short phrases`
- `Simple sentences`
- `Connected speaking`

### C. Can the child read Kannada?

Check:
- letters
- simple words
- short sentences

Mark one:
- `Not yet`
- `Some letters`
- `Words`
- `Sentences and more`

### D. Can the child write Kannada?

Check:
- a few letters
- a word
- a short sentence

Mark one:
- `Not yet`
- `Some letters`
- `Words`
- `Sentences and more`

## 2. Fast Placement Guide

### `ಮಾತು ಕಲಿ`

Place here if the child:
- understands little Kannada
- speaks very little or only isolated words
- is not ready for reading/writing yet

### `ಅಕ್ಷರ ಕಲಿ`

Place here if the child:
- understands simple Kannada
- speaks a little in words or short phrases
- is ready to learn letters

### `ಪದ ಕಲಿ`

Place here if the child:
- knows many letters
- can read some simple words
- can say short sentences

### `ವಾಕ್ಯ ಕಲಿ`

Place here if the child:
- reads words and short sentences
- writes some sentences
- can hold a short guided conversation

### `ಕನ್ನಡ ಮಾತುಗಾರರು`

Place here if the child:
- speaks comfortably in Kannada
- reads short passages
- writes connected sentences or short paragraphs
- can explain ideas in Kannada

## 3. Super Quick Decision Table

- `Understands little + speaks little` -> `ಮಾತು ಕಲಿ`
- `Speaks a little + starting letters` -> `ಅಕ್ಷರ ಕಲಿ`
- `Knows letters + reads some words` -> `ಪದ ಕಲಿ`
- `Reads/writes short sentences` -> `ವಾಕ್ಯ ಕಲಿ`
- `Speaks confidently + explains ideas` -> `ಕನ್ನಡ ಮಾತುಗಾರರು`

## 4. Notes For Volunteers

- Place by skill, not age.
- Do not worry about perfect pronunciation.
- If unsure between two levels, choose the lower one and reassess after a few weeks.
- A confident child with weaker reading can still belong in a lower level if speaking is not yet steady.
- Keep the interaction relaxed. A nervous child may know more than they show in the first minute.
