# Level 2 Weekly Plan: Weeks 1-8

Level: `ಅಕ್ಷರ ಕಲಿ`

Audience:
- children who can follow simple Kannada classroom routines
- children ready to begin or strengthen Kannada letter learning

Teaching approach:
- continue speaking and listening
- introduce letters slowly and repeatedly
- connect sounds, words, and simple meaning

Suggested class length: `45-60 minutes`

Suggested class structure:
- `10 min` spoken warm-up and review
- `15 min` letter focus
- `10 min` word practice
- `10 min` game, reading practice, or mini activity
- `5 min` wrap-up and home task

## Week 1: Welcome Back to Kannada + First Letters

Theme:
- classroom routines and first swaras

Goals:
- settle into class routines
- recognize and say `ಅ`, `ಆ`
- connect sound to simple familiar words

Letters:
- ಅ
- ಆ

Vocabulary:
- ಅಮ್ಮ
- ಆನೆ

Useful phrases:
- ಇದು ಯಾವ ಅಕ್ಷರ?
- ಇದು ಅ
- ಇದು ಆ

Activities:
- air-writing letters
- trace on paper or sand tray
- picture match: `ಅ` / `ಆ`

Home practice:
- find `ಅ` and `ಆ` in the alphabet page

## Week 2: More Swarās

Theme:
- short and long vowel pairs

Goals:
- recognize and say `ಇ`, `ಈ`, `ಉ`, `ಊ`
- notice short vs long sounds

Letters:
- ಇ
- ಈ
- ಉ
- ಊ

Vocabulary:
- ಇಲಿ
- ಈಚೆ
- ಉಪ್ಪು
- ಊರು

Activities:
- vowel sound echo game
- letter card sorting
- match picture to first sound

Home practice:
- say the letters aloud and identify one word for each

## Week 3: E and O Family Swarās

Theme:
- vowel families

Goals:
- recognize and say `ಎ`, `ಏ`, `ಐ`, `ಒ`, `ಓ`, `ಔ`
- hear the difference between related sounds

Letters:
- ಎ
- ಏ
- ಐ
- ಒ
- ಓ
- ಔ

Vocabulary:
- ಎಲೆ
- ಏಣಿ
- ಐದು
- ಒಂಟೆ
- ಓಟ
- ಔಷಧ

Activities:
- listen and point
- picture-to-letter matching
- group reading of vowel cards

Home practice:
- review all swaras learned so far

## Week 4: First Consonants

Theme:
- beginning with `ಕ` and `ಗ`

Goals:
- identify first consonants
- connect consonants to spoken words

Letters:
- ಕ
- ಗ

Vocabulary:
- ಕಮಲ
- ಕಾಯಿ
- ಗಿಡ
- ಗಜ

Useful phrases:
- ಇದು ಕ
- ಇದು ಗ
- ಕ ಎಂದು ಹೇಳು

Activities:
- tracing
- flashcard reading
- object sorting by first sound

Home practice:
- look for words beginning with `ಕ` and `ಗ`

## Week 5: More Consonants

Theme:
- `ಚ`, `ಜ`, `ಟ`, `ಡ`

Goals:
- identify these letters
- hear the sound difference in familiar words

Letters:
- ಚ
- ಜ
- ಟ
- ಡ

Vocabulary:
- ಚಪ್ಪಲಿ
- ಜಮಖಾನ
- ಟಮೋಟ
- ಡಬ್ಬಿ

Activities:
- first-sound picture game
- letter bingo
- group reading of the day’s letters

Home practice:
- choose one letter and bring one matching word next class

## Week 6: Everyday Consonants

Theme:
- `ತ`, `ದ`, `ನ`, `ಪ`, `ಬ`, `ಮ`

Goals:
- identify and say very common consonants
- use them in familiar words

Letters:
- ತ
- ದ
- ನ
- ಪ
- ಬ
- ಮ

Vocabulary:
- ತಾತ
- ದೋಸೆ
- ನಾಯಿ
- ಪುಸ್ತಕ
- ಬಾಳೆಹಣ್ಣು
- ಮಣ್ಣು

Activities:
- word-picture match
- say the first letter before the full word
- pair work: pick card and read sound

Home practice:
- say 3 words at home and identify the first letter

## Week 7: Bringing Letters Together

Theme:
- review of letters learned

Goals:
- read letters more quickly
- build very simple letter-to-word confidence
- introduce the idea that letters join to make words

Review letters:
- swaras learned
- consonants from Weeks 4-6

Activities:
- quick recognition round
- mix-and-match cards
- read simple words like:
  - ಅಮ್ಮ
  - ಆನೆ
  - ನಾಯಿ
  - ಗಿಡ

Home practice:
- read 5 letter cards and 3 words

## Week 8: Beginning Kagunita Awareness

Theme:
- pattern noticing with `ಕ` row

Goals:
- understand that one consonant changes shape with different vowel sounds
- notice the pattern, not master it yet

Focus:
- ಕ
- ಕಾ
- ಕಿ
- ಕೀ
- ಕು
- ಕೂ
- ಕೆ
- ಕೇ

Activities:
- say the row aloud together
- spot what changes in the shape
- use the kagunita reference page

Teacher language:
- `ಇದು ಕ`
- `ಇದು ಕಾ`
- `ಬದಲಾದ ಆಕಾರ ನೋಡಿ`

Home practice:
- review the `ಕ` row with a parent

## Teacher Notes

- Keep spoken Kannada active every week.
- Do not let letter learning become only worksheet-based.
- Use tracing, pointing, matching, and saying aloud.
- Encourage children to say sounds confidently before asking them to write perfectly.
- Revisit old letters every week; do not teach and forget.
