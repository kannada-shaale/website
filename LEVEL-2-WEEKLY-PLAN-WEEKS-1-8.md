# Level 2 Teacher-Ready Weekly Plan: Weeks 1-8

Level: `ಅಕ್ಷರ ಕಲಿ`

Audience:
- children who can follow simple Kannada classroom routines
- children ready to connect spoken Kannada with letters and early reading

Teaching goal:
- keep speaking confidence active while introducing letters
- connect sound, symbol, and meaning
- move slowly enough that children notice patterns, not just memorize shapes

Suggested class length: `45-60 minutes`

## Teacher Prep

Materials to keep ready every week:
- alphabet cards
- picture cards for target words
- sand tray, whiteboard, or tracing sheet
- crayons or pencils
- pocket chart or floor cards for sorting
- quick take-home note for parents

Suggested weekly routine:
- `8 min` spoken warm-up and review
- `12 min` letter introduction and sound practice
- `10 min` tracing, pointing, or matching
- `10 min` simple word reading or game
- `5-10 min` recap, quick check, and home practice

Teacher reminders:
- always say the sound clearly before asking children to identify the letter
- keep old letters visible while teaching new ones
- use spoken words children already know whenever possible
- avoid too much worksheet time in one stretch

## Week 1: Welcome Back to Kannada + First Letters

Theme:
- classroom routines and first swaras

Objectives:
- settle into class routines
- recognize and say `ಅ` and `ಆ`
- connect letters with familiar words

Letters:
- ಅ
- ಆ

Vocabulary:
- ಅಮ್ಮ
- ಆನೆ

Teacher phrases:
- ಇದು ಯಾವ ಅಕ್ಷರ?
- ಇದು ಅ
- ಇದು ಆ
- ಅ ಅಮ್ಮ
- ಆ ಆನೆ

Materials:
- large `ಅ` and `ಆ` cards
- picture cards for `ಅಮ್ಮ` and `ಆನೆ`
- tray for air-writing or finger tracing

Lesson flow:
1. Begin with greetings and one familiar spoken routine.
2. Show `ಅ` and say the sound clearly. Repeat with `ಆ`.
3. Connect each letter to one anchor word and picture.
4. Ask children to trace the letter in the air, on the table, or in sand.
5. Play a quick match game: letter card to picture card.
6. End by asking each child to identify one of the two letters.

Quick check:
- can the child distinguish `ಅ` from `ಆ`?
- can the child connect one anchor picture to the correct letter?

Home practice:
- find `ಅ` and `ಆ` on the alphabet page and say one word for each

## Week 2: More Swarās

Theme:
- short and long vowel pairs

Objectives:
- recognize and say `ಇ`, `ಈ`, `ಉ`, `ಊ`
- hear the difference between short and long sounds
- connect each swara to a familiar word

Letters:
- ಇ
- ಈ
- ಉ
- ಊ

Vocabulary:
- ಇಲಿ
- ಈಚೆ
- ಉಪ್ಪು
- ಊರು

Teacher phrases:
- ಇದು ಇ
- ಇದು ಈ
- ಚಿಕ್ಕ ಧ್ವನಿ
- ಉದ್ದ ಧ್ವನಿ
- ಯಾವ ಪದ?

Materials:
- swara cards
- picture cards for all four words
- sorting mat for short vs long

Lesson flow:
1. Review `ಅ` and `ಆ` before teaching new letters.
2. Introduce `ಇ` and `ಈ` as a pair, then `ಉ` and `ಊ`.
3. Model short and long sounds dramatically so children can hear the contrast.
4. Sort picture cards under the matching letter.
5. Let children echo-read the four letters in rhythm.
6. Close with a point-and-say round.

Quick check:
- can the child repeat all four sounds?
- can the child match at least two pictures correctly?

Home practice:
- say the four letters aloud and identify one word for each

## Week 3: E and O Family Swarās

Theme:
- expanding the swara family

Objectives:
- recognize and say `ಎ`, `ಏ`, `ಐ`, `ಒ`, `ಓ`, `ಔ`
- listen carefully to related sound groups
- review all swaras taught so far

Letters:
- ಎ
- ಏ
- ಐ
- ಒ
- ಓ
- ಔ

Vocabulary:
- ಎಲೆ
- ಏಣಿ
- ಐದು
- ಒಂಟೆ
- ಓಟ
- ಔಷಧ

Teacher phrases:
- ಕೇಳಿ ಮತ್ತು ತೋರಿಸು
- ಇದು ಏ
- ಇದು ಓ
- ಯಾವ ಧ್ವನಿ ಕೇಳಿದೆ?

Materials:
- swara cards
- six picture cards
- pocket chart or floor grid

Lesson flow:
1. Review all previous swaras with a quick chant.
2. Teach the new letters in pairs or families so children hear the pattern.
3. Use listen-and-point activities to check sound discrimination.
4. Match pictures to first sounds as a group.
5. Do a whole-class swara reading strip from start to finish.
6. End with a short game where children pick a card and say the sound.

Quick check:
- can the child point to the correct letter when hearing the sound?
- does the child recognize some of the related pairs?

Home practice:
- review all swaras learned so far for 3-5 minutes

## Week 4: First Consonants

Theme:
- beginning vyanjanas with familiar words

Objectives:
- identify `ಕ` and `ಗ`
- connect consonants to words children may already know
- notice that these letters begin many useful words

Letters:
- ಕ
- ಗ

Vocabulary:
- ಕಮಲ
- ಕಾಯಿ
- ಗಿಡ
- ಗಜ

Teacher phrases:
- ಇದು ಕ
- ಇದು ಗ
- ಕ ಎಂದು ಹೇಳು
- ಗ ಎಲ್ಲಿ ಇದೆ?

Materials:
- `ಕ` and `ಗ` cards
- object or picture cards
- tracing sheet

Lesson flow:
1. Review swaras briefly so children stay connected to earlier learning.
2. Introduce `ಕ` and `ಗ` with strong visual contrast.
3. Model the sound and have children repeat.
4. Match words or pictures to the correct starting letter.
5. Trace both letters with finger, marker, or crayon.
6. Close with a mini sort: `ಕ` pile and `ಗ` pile.

Quick check:
- can the child identify `ಕ` and `ಗ` when shown side by side?
- can the child say one word for either letter?

Home practice:
- look for words beginning with `ಕ` and `ಗ`

## Week 5: More Consonants

Theme:
- adding `ಚ`, `ಜ`, `ಟ`, `ಡ`

Objectives:
- identify four new consonants
- hear the first sound in familiar words
- keep older consonants active through review

Letters:
- ಚ
- ಜ
- ಟ
- ಡ

Vocabulary:
- ಚಪ್ಪಲಿ
- ಜಮಖಾನ
- ಟಮೋಟ
- ಡಬ್ಬಿ

Teacher phrases:
- ಮೊದಲ ಅಕ್ಷರ ಯಾವುದು?
- ಇದು ಚ
- ಇದು ಜ
- ಕೇಳಿ ಮತ್ತು ಹೊಂದಿಸು

Materials:
- four letter cards
- matching word and picture cards
- bingo board or quick sort mat

Lesson flow:
1. Review `ಕ` and `ಗ`.
2. Introduce the four new letters with pictures and clear sound modeling.
3. Ask children to repeat each sound and anchor word.
4. Play a first-sound picture game.
5. Run a simple letter bingo or card hunt.
6. Close by mixing all six consonants learned so far for quick recognition.

Quick check:
- can the child identify at least 2 of the 4 new consonants?
- can the child match one picture to its starting sound?

Home practice:
- choose one new letter and bring one matching word next class

## Week 6: Everyday Consonants

Theme:
- high-use consonants in common words

Objectives:
- identify `ತ`, `ದ`, `ನ`, `ಪ`, `ಬ`, `ಮ`
- say the first sound before saying the whole word
- build confidence reading familiar beginnings

Letters:
- ತ
- ದ
- ನ
- ಪ
- ಬ
- ಮ

Vocabulary:
- ತಾತ
- ದೋಸೆ
- ನಾಯಿ
- ಪುಸ್ತಕ
- ಬಾಳೆಹಣ್ಣು
- ಮಣ್ಣು

Teacher phrases:
- ಮೊದಲು ಅಕ್ಷರ ಹೇಳು
- ಈಗ ಸಂಪೂರ್ಣ ಪದ ಹೇಳು
- ನಾಯಿ ಯಾವ ಅಕ್ಷರದಿಂದ ಆರಂಭ?

Materials:
- six letter cards
- picture cards
- pocket chart

Lesson flow:
1. Start with a quick review of all previous consonants.
2. Teach the six new letters in two or three small groups.
3. Say the sound, then the word, then ask children to echo both.
4. Match pictures to their first letters.
5. Let children pick one card and say the sound before the word.
6. End with a whole-group rapid review.

Quick check:
- can the child identify at least 3 of the 6 letters?
- can the child hear the first sound in a familiar word?

Home practice:
- say 3 words at home and identify the first letter

## Week 7: Bringing Letters Together

Theme:
- reading simple familiar words

Objectives:
- review all letters learned so far
- build faster recognition
- help children experience success reading simple words

Practice words:
- ಅಮ್ಮ
- ಆನೆ
- ನಾಯಿ
- ಗಿಡ

Teacher phrases:
- ಅಕ್ಷರಗಳನ್ನು ಸೇರಿಸೋಣ
- ಇದು ಯಾವ ಪದ?
- ನಿಧಾನವಾಗಿ ಓದು

Materials:
- mixed letter cards
- simple word cards
- pointer or highlighting strip

Lesson flow:
1. Review letters with a rapid flashcard round.
2. Show how letters come together to make a word.
3. Read simple words as a group, one sound at a time.
4. Use mix-and-match cards to rebuild a familiar word.
5. Give each child one word card to attempt with support.
6. End with praise focused on effort and trying.

Quick check:
- can the child read or repeat one simple word?
- does the child recognize more letters more quickly now?

Home practice:
- read 5 letter cards and 3 simple words

## Week 8: Beginning Kagunita Awareness

Theme:
- noticing the `ಕ` row pattern

Objectives:
- understand that one consonant changes with different vowel sounds
- say the `ಕ` row with support
- notice the visual pattern without pressure to master all forms

Focus:
- ಕ
- ಕಾ
- ಕಿ
- ಕೀ
- ಕು
- ಕೂ
- ಕೆ
- ಕೇ

Teacher phrases:
- ಇದು ಕ
- ಇದು ಕಾ
- ಬದಲಾದ ಆಕಾರ ನೋಡಿ
- ಒಂದೇ ಅಕ್ಷರ, ಬೇರೆ ಧ್ವನಿ

Materials:
- kagunita chart for the `ಕ` row
- pointer
- matching row strip for students

Lesson flow:
1. Review `ಕ` from earlier weeks.
2. Show the `ಕ` row one form at a time.
3. Say the row together in rhythm.
4. Ask children what looks the same and what changes.
5. Match spoken forms to written cards.
6. End by reminding children that pattern noticing is the goal for now.

Quick check:
- does the child understand that the base letter stays the same?
- can the child repeat at least part of the row confidently?

Home practice:
- review the `ಕ` row with a parent using the kagunita page

## End-of-Cycle Teacher Notes

Use these 8 weeks to identify:
- children who need more time with letter recognition
- children ready to move into steady word building
- children who are already beginning to read short familiar words confidently

Placement signal:
- if a child can identify many of the taught letters, connect letters to sounds, and attempt simple word reading, they are progressing well in `ಅಕ್ಷರ ಕಲಿ`
